What We Run

02 / 05

Four Services.
One Operating Layer.

These are four services, not four steps. A client can start with any one of them, and most end up using more than one because the operating layer is where they overlap.

Operations Management

We take ownership of the day-to-day operating engine: the routines, the hand-offs, the exceptions and the reporting. Not advice on the side — accountability for the work itself.

  • Daily operating rhythm and escalation paths
  • Named ownership for every recurring process
  • Weekly operating report to the leadership team

Workflow Optimisation

Processes mapped as they actually run, measured against the outcome they are meant to produce, then rebuilt. We remove steps before we automate them, because automating waste only makes it faster.

  • End-to-end process mapping and cycle-time analysis
  • Standard operating procedures people will actually follow
  • Automation where it earns its place

Systems Integration

Finance, CRM, operations and reporting brought into one connected estate, with a single system of record. One number for one thing, wherever in the business you ask.

  • Integration design and data-ownership mapping
  • Migration, reconciliation and cut-over management
  • Ongoing monitoring of every connection

Strategic Support

Operating-model design, capacity planning and the numbers behind the decision. We sit close enough to the work that the recommendation and the reality are the same thing.

  • Operating model and org design
  • Capacity, cost-to-serve and margin modelling
  • Board-ready operational reporting
